Z Horde Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 6, 2026

Wow Games Ltd. ("Wow Games," "we," "us," or "our") develops the mobile game Z Horde: Escape the Highway ("Z Horde" or the "Service"), available on Google Play under the package name com.wowgames.zhorde. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you play Z Horde, how it is used and shared, and the choices available to you.

Publishing Partner

Z Horde is developed in cooperation with our publishing partner YSO Corp. The game includes YSO Corp's GameUtils software development kit (SDK), which provides analytics, privacy-consent, and advertising-mediation features. Information collected through this SDK is processed by YSO Corp and the third-party providers listed below, under their own privacy policies.

Audience

Z Horde is intended for users who are at least 13 years old and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through Z Horde, contact us so that we can investigate and take appropriate action.

Information We Collect or Share

We and our service providers may collect or receive the following categories of information, depending on the version of the game and the services enabled:

  • Device and other identifiers: advertising identifiers (such as GAID), app instance identifiers, device identifiers, and identifiers generated by analytics, advertising, or attribution providers.
  • Approximate location: a general location inferred from an IP address. Z Horde does not request precise GPS location.
  • App activity: app launches, session length, level starts, level wins and losses, gameplay and feature interactions, and other in-game events. If advertising is enabled in a version of the game, this may also include ad impressions, clicks, and completions.
  • Diagnostics and performance: device model, operating-system version, app version, SDK status, network diagnostics, and crash or error information when a provider's reporting feature is enabled.

Z Horde does not provide user accounts and does not ask you to submit a name, email address, password, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, audio files, or documents during normal gameplay. Game progress, such as your current level, owned weapons, and in-game cash, is stored locally on your device.

How We Use Information

We and our providers may use information to:

  • operate, maintain, and test the game and its consent and related features;
  • measure gameplay, engagement, retention, and game balance;
  • attribute installs and in-game events to marketing campaigns;
  • provide and measure advertising, where advertising is enabled and permitted;
  • diagnose technical problems and improve performance; and
  • prevent fraud, protect security, comply with law, and enforce policies.

Consent and Privacy Choices

Z Horde presents privacy choices where required, including in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, and applicable U.S. states. Depending on your location, you may be able to consent, refuse consent, or opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for targeted advertising. You can review and change these choices at any time through the Settings button in the game, which opens the data privacy options.

Refusing consent does not necessarily stop all data processing. Limited data may still be processed to operate the game, maintain security, prevent fraud, or comply with law. You can also reset or delete your advertising identifier in your device settings.

Third-Party Services

Third-party SDKs may collect information directly from your device and process it under their own privacy policies. Z Horde uses some or all of the following services:

  • YSO Corp (GameUtils SDK)
  • Google Play Services
  • Meta (Facebook SDK)
  • AppLovin MAX
  • Unity

Some providers act as service providers on our behalf. Marketing and attribution partners may also receive data as independent third parties or receive campaign postbacks. Under some privacy laws, these transfers may be considered a "sale" or "sharing" even when no money is paid for the personal information itself. If we add advertising networks in a future version of Z Horde, we will update this list.

Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, including analytics, attribution, fraud prevention, legal compliance, and dispute resolution. Third-party providers determine retention periods for information they process under their own policies. Game progress stored on your device is deleted when you uninstall Z Horde or clear its app data.

Security and International Transfers

Data transmitted by the integrated services is encrypted in transit. No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Information may be processed in countries other than the country where you live, subject to applicable safeguards.

Your Privacy Rights and Data Requests

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or a copy of your personal information, or to object to or opt out of certain processing. Z Horde does not provide user accounts. To submit a request, email peter@wowgames.us with the game name (Z Horde), platform, country or state, and the type of request. Do not send passwords or payment information. We may ask for limited information needed to locate the relevant SDK or advertising identifier and verify the request.

You may also exercise choices directly through the in-game Settings, your device advertising settings, or the privacy controls offered by the applicable third-party provider.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to Z Horde, its providers, or legal requirements. We will post the revised policy on this page and update the effective date above.

Contact Us

For questions, privacy requests, or complaints, contact Wow Games Ltd. at peter@wowgames.us.
